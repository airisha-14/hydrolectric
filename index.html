HydrolectricCisadane River

The Hydrolectric is a water turbine system designed to harness the energy of moving river water and generate clean electricity for the residents of Desa Lengkong Kyai.

Our Idea

Many households near the Cisadane River in Desa Lengkong Kyai face unstable access to electricity. Since the river flows continuously, our product—the Hydrolectric—converts the water’s natural kinetic energy into a reliable, sustainable power source for the local community.

How It Works

1. Water Flows

Flowing water from the Cisadane River pushes directly against the Hydrolectric turbine blades.

2. Turbine Spins

The spinning blades turn a central shaft connected to an electric generator.

3. Electricity Is Made

The generator produces clean electricity to power lights and essential electronics in Desa Lengkong Kyai.

Why Hydrolectric Is Useful for Desa Lengkong Kyai

  • Harnesses the continuous flow of the Cisadane River to provide a 24/7 renewable energy source.
  • Produces zero emissions or pollution, preserving the local environment of Desa Lengkong Kyai.
  • Provides reliable nighttime illumination for students studying and families conducting daily tasks.
  • Utilizes accessible natural resources to deliver a cost-effective, sustainable energy solution.
